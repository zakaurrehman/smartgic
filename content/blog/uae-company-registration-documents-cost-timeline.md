Registering a company in the UAE is faster than almost anywhere in the world — but only when the application is prepared correctly. Here is exactly what registration involves in 2026: the documents, the real costs and a realistic timeline.

## The documents you actually need

For most mainland and free zone registrations:

- **Passport copies** of all shareholders and the manager
- **Passport-size photographs** (white background)
- **Proposed trade names** — prepare 2–3 options in case your first choice is taken
- **Business activity selection** — the licensed activities you will operate under
- **UAE entry stamp or visa page** if you are already in the country

That is genuinely it for most activities. You do **not** need a business plan, attested degrees or upfront share capital deposits for standard commercial and professional licences. Regulated activities (healthcare, education, financial services) carry extra approvals.

## The registration steps

1. **Trade name reservation** — your name is checked and reserved with the authority
2. **Initial approval** — the green light to proceed with your activity
3. **MOA signing** — for mainland companies, the Memorandum of Association is drafted and notarised
4. **Office/address** — an Ejari tenancy (mainland) or flexi-desk package (free zone)
5. **Licence issuance** — pay the fees, receive your trade licence
6. **Post-licence** — establishment card, visa processing and the corporate bank account

## What it costs in 2026

| Setup type | Realistic starting cost |
|---|---|
| Free zone (1 visa) | From AED 5,750 |
| Premium free zone (multi-activity) | From AED 12,900 |
| Mainland (DET) | From AED 18,900 |

Watch for quotes that quietly exclude government fees, establishment cards or visa costs — the cheap headline number grows quickly. Always ask for an **all-inclusive, itemised quote**.

## How long does it take?

- **Trade name + initial approval:** 1–2 working days
- **Free zone licence:** 3–5 working days total
- **Mainland licence:** about a week once the tenancy is ready
- **Residence visa after licence:** 5–10 working days
- **Corporate bank account:** 1–4 weeks depending on the bank

So a founder starting from zero can realistically be **licensed, visa'd and banked inside a month**.

## Common registration mistakes

- Choosing an activity code that doesn't match what you actually do (banks notice)
- Picking a jurisdiction before comparing visa quotas and market access
- Signing a flexi-desk deal that can't scale when you need more visas
- Leaving the bank account until last minute without a KYC-ready file
