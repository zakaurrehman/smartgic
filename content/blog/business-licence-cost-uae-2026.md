Search "UAE business licence cost" and you'll find numbers from AED 5,000 to AED 50,000 — all technically true, none telling the full story. Here is the honest breakdown of what you will actually pay in 2026.

## The headline numbers

| Setup | Realistic all-in first year |
|---|---|
| Free zone, 0–1 visa | **AED 5,750 – 12,000** |
| Free zone, multi-activity / more visas | **AED 12,900 – 25,000** |
| Mainland (DET) commercial | **AED 18,900 – 35,000** |
| Mainland professional | **AED 12,000 – 20,000** |

The ranges are wide because your **activity, visa count and office choice** drive the total more than the licence itself.

## What makes up the cost

1. **Licence & registration fees** — the authority's charges for the licence, name and registration
2. **Office / address** — flexi-desk (from ~AED 5–8k in free zones) or Ejari tenancy (mainland; your biggest variable)
3. **Establishment card** — required to sponsor visas
4. **Visas** — entry permit, medical, Emirates ID and stamping per person (~AED 3,500–6,000 each all-in)
5. **MOA notarisation** — mainland companies
6. **Third-party approvals** — only for regulated activities

## The fees cheap quotes hide

That AED 5,500 ad usually excludes:

- The **establishment/immigration card** (needed before any visa)
- **Visa costs** — "1 visa allocation" means eligibility, not a processed visa
- **Medical + Emirates ID** fees
- **Security deposits** some zones charge
- Year-2 renewal at a much higher "standard" rate than the promo year

Rule of thumb: if a quote isn't **itemised with government fees shown separately**, it isn't a quote — it's bait.

## Annual running costs to budget

- Licence renewal (roughly the original licence fee)
- Office/desk package renewal
- Visa renewals every 2 years
- Corporate Tax registration is free, but **bookkeeping/filing** has a cost — budget from AED 3–8k/year for a small company

A lean free zone consultancy realistically runs on **AED 12–18k per year** all-in after setup; a mainland trading company more.

## Ways to genuinely reduce cost

- Choose a zone whose **package matches your visa needs** — don't pay for 6 visa slots to use one
- Start with a **flexi-desk**, upgrade when you hire
- Bundle licence + visa + bank into one engagement to avoid re-work fees
- Renew on time — fines are the most avoidable cost in the UAE
