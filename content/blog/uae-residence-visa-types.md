Every UAE residence visa answers one question differently: **who sponsors you?** A company you own, an employer, a family member — or, with the Golden Visa, effectively yourself. Here is the 2026 map of the main routes and who each one fits.

## Investor / Partner Visa

**Sponsor:** your own company.
The standard route for founders — own shares in a UAE company (mainland or free zone) and the licence sponsors your residency.

- **Validity:** typically 2 years, renewable
- **Needs:** trade licence, establishment card, medical, Emirates ID
- **Fits:** entrepreneurs, freelancers-turned-founders, overseas investors

## Employment Visa

**Sponsor:** your employer.
The classic expat route — MOHRE work permit, labour contract, then residence stamping.

- **Validity:** usually 2 years, tied to the job
- **Fits:** salaried professionals; the employer handles costs and paperwork

## Family / Dependant Visa

**Sponsor:** a resident family member.
Residents meeting the salary criteria (generally AED 4,000+) can sponsor a spouse, children — and in many cases parents.

- **Validity:** matches the sponsor's visa
- **Needs:** attested marriage/birth certificates ([we handle attestation](/services/document-clearing-services)), tenancy, salary proof

## Golden Visa (10-Year)

**Sponsor:** none — that's the point.
Long-term residency for investors (AED 2M+ in property or funds), entrepreneurs, skilled professionals (AED 30k+ salary with degree), scientists, talents and top students.

- **Validity:** 10 years, renewable; family included; no re-entry time limits
- Full eligibility breakdown in our [Golden Visa guide](/blog/uae-golden-visa-guide-2026)

## Green Visa (5-Year)

The middle path — self-sponsored 5-year residency for skilled employees (AED 15k+ salary, bachelor's degree) and freelancers/self-employed with proven income (AED 360k+ over two years).

- **Fits:** established professionals who don't yet clear Golden thresholds

## Retirement Visa

For the 55+ crowd: qualify via AED 1M savings, AED 20k+ monthly income, or AED 1M property.
- **Validity:** 5 years, renewable

## Which visa should you pursue?

| Your situation | Best route |
|---|---|
| Starting/owning a company | Investor visa |
| UAE job offer | Employment visa |
| Spouse/parent is a resident | Family visa |
| AED 2M+ to invest, or top-tier salary | Golden Visa |
| Skilled but below Golden bar | Green Visa |
| Retiring to the sun | Retirement visa |

Most founders' cheapest fast route is the **investor visa via their own licence** — then upgrading to Golden once eligibility is met.
