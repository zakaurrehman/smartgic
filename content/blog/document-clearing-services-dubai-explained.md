Behind every licence, visa and court filing in the UAE sits a layer of paperwork most people never see done well: typing centres, attestations, translations and stamps in the right order. That layer is **document clearing** — and it's the licensed activity at the core of Smartgic. Here's what it actually covers and when you'll need it.

## What document clearing includes

### 1. Government application typing
UAE authorities require applications typed in prescribed Arabic formats through licensed channels — visa forms, licence applications, labour contracts, status changes. One wrong field and the application bounces, costing days. Clearing specialists type and submit hundreds of these weekly, so they don't bounce.

### 2. Attestation & legalisation
Making a document legally valid across borders. A foreign degree used in the UAE typically travels through:

1. Notary/authority in the issuing country
2. UAE Embassy in that country
3. **MOFA (Ministry of Foreign Affairs)** attestation in the UAE

The same chain (reversed) applies to UAE documents used abroad. Common cases: degrees for employment visas, marriage/birth certificates for [family sponsorship](/blog/uae-residence-visa-types), commercial documents and POAs for [company deals](/blog/transfer-company-shares-dubai-llc).

### 3. Certified legal translation
Courts and government departments accept only certified translations. Contracts, certificates and court documents — Arabic ↔ English and 12+ languages — stamped by licensed legal translators.

### 4. Notary & POA processing
Drafting and processing powers of attorney, declarations and undertakings before the notary public — including remote signings where permitted.

## When you'll actually need it

- **Visa applications** — attested certificates for family visas and skilled-visa categories
- **Company formation** — attested parent-company documents for branches and corporate shareholders
- **Banking** — attested and translated documents for compliance files
- **Court matters** — translated, attested evidence and contracts
- **Property & inheritance** — POAs and attested personal documents

## How long does attestation take?

- **UAE-issued documents:** often 1–3 working days through MOFA
- **Foreign documents:** depends on the issuing country's chain — typically 1–3 weeks; express options exist for many countries

Start attestation *before* the deadline that needs it — it's the step people discover late.

## What it costs

MOFA attestation runs a fixed government fee per document (roughly AED 150–200 for most personal documents), plus embassy fees that vary by country, plus service charges. Certified translation is priced per page. A typical degree attestation lands in the **AED 400–900** range all-in; commercial documents more.
