Dubai has hundreds of business setup consultants — from licensed professionals to Instagram accounts reselling licences with a markup. Choosing the wrong one costs you more than money: wrong structures, surprise fees and stalled visas. Here is how to tell them apart.

## First check: are they actually licensed?

A legitimate consultant operates under a UAE trade licence for this work — document clearing, management consultancy or PRO services. Ask for the **licence number** and verify it on the DET website. If they hesitate, walk.

## The questions that reveal everything

Ask these on the first call:

1. **"What's the all-inclusive price, itemised?"** — the only acceptable answer shows government fees and service fees separately, in writing
2. **"Which jurisdiction do you recommend and *why not* the others?"** — good consultants compare; bad ones push whichever free zone pays them the highest commission
3. **"What happens after the licence?"** — visas, bank account, tax registration, renewals. If the answer stops at the licence, they're selling paper, not setup
4. **"Who handles my file — you or a subcontractor?"** — you want a named person who answers your messages
5. **"What's *not* included?"** — the honest ones tell you before you sign

## Red flags to walk away from

- **"Guaranteed bank account"** — no one can guarantee a bank's compliance decision; honest consultants quote approval *rates*, not certainties
- **Prices that look impossible** — an AED 5,500 ad that becomes AED 15,000 by licence day is the industry's oldest trick ([see the real cost breakdown](/blog/business-licence-cost-uae-2026))
- **Pressure to decide today** — authority prices don't expire tonight; urgency is a sales tactic
- **No physical office or licence shown** — you're dealing with a middleman's middleman
- **One-zone shops** — if they only sell one free zone, every problem looks like that free zone

## What fair pricing looks like

Consultant service fees (on top of government costs) typically run **AED 1,500–5,000** for a straightforward setup. Dramatically less usually means hidden margins in "government fees"; dramatically more should come with genuinely complex structuring.
